Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Pearl Stream Uganda ("the App") is a streaming application that provides access to movies and live TV content. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, and app version, used for compatibility and crash diagnostics.
  • Usage data: general app interactions (screens viewed, features used) to help us improve the App.
  • Network and security data: the App performs VPN detection and SSL certificate pinning to protect streaming content and maintain service security. This may involve checking network connection characteristics, but does not identify you personally.
  • We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, or precise location unless you voluntarily provide it (for example, when contacting support).

How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App's streaming functionality.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access.
  • To diagnose technical issues and fix bugs.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share data with third parties except where required to operate core services (such as content delivery infrastructure) or where required by law.

Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party infrastructure providers to deliver streaming content. These providers may process technical data (such as IP address) solely to deliver content to your device.

Data Security

The App uses industry-standard security measures, including SSL certificate pinning, to protect data in transit between your device and our servers.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

You can uninstall the App at any time to stop all data collection. If you have questions about your data, contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact us
For privacy questions or requests, reach out via the contact details listed on the Pearl Stream Uganda Play Store listing.
